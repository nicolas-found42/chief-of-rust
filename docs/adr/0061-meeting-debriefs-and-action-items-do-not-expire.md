# Meeting Debriefs and Action Items do not expire

An Executive Assistant Run completes after it extracts the Meeting Debrief and materializes its Action Items. The Meeting Debrief remains available, and each pending Action Item remains pending until the workspace owner promotes or dismisses it; neither is skipped, resolved or deleted merely because thirty days pass. Old pending work may be visually de-emphasized but never changed automatically.

Creating the Gmail draft remains an explicit action gated by recipient validation and has no time limit. Together with ADR-0053, this supersedes ADR-0038's blocked Run, thirty-day expiry, and coupled outward-write lifecycle while preserving its privacy rule that the app never creates an unreviewed email draft.
