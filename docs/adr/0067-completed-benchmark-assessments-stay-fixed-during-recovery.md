# Completed benchmark assessments stay fixed during recovery

Intermittent judge failures can leave an otherwise retained research population incompletely
assessed. Reassessing every person repeatedly would replace valid adverse results as well as
failures, making improvement vulnerable to selecting a more favorable response. Failed-only
reassessment therefore preserves the first complete person and collection assessments, including
zero recovery, uncertainty and overclaims, and judges only unfinished entries.

Reuse requires unchanged references, population, judge configuration and the exact public evidence
bundle. Reports retain a bounded, verified parent lineage and distinguish carried results from new
assessment attempts. Earlier failures remain inspectable. Unattested legacy reports require one
full reassessment before reuse; generated reports and snapshots must keep their bytes unchanged.
Research outcomes remain frozen: this recovers evaluation, not interrupted research or missing
sources. A comparison still requires complete assessment of the same population in both arms.

A reference verdict is provisional until support/usefulness assessment completes. Later failure
preserves its cited evidence without granting positive recovery credit. Findings with unresolved
claim identities or statement text remain reviewable uncertainty, rather than disappearing into
a clean assessment or becoming established identity failures. This extends ADR-0063; it does not
relax ADR-0066's per-request model deadlines or substitute judge recovery for live quality evidence.
