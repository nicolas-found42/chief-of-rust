# Content Packs may be supported by one Source Item

A Content Opportunity is supported by one or more Source Items, so its Opportunity Brief now has a
floor of one and records the supporting Source Item count explicitly. A floor of three prevented
Content Scout from creating any Content Pack with the real source profile, while a floor of two
would still reject a valid single-source Content Opportunity; the visible count lets a person judge
the breadth of support without treating breadth as a hidden completion gate.
