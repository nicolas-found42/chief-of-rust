# Person Profiles are automatically researched dossiers

Person Profiles become Workspace-owned dossiers assembled from retained source documents, grounded
claims, and shared records of a person's work and connections; readable summaries organise these
records without replacing them. Creation and research proceed automatically from user input,
Calendar, and sufficiently established Transcript identities, with bounded persistent work and
progressive results, because maintaining the record is the application's responsibility.

This supersedes the automatic-creation prohibition in spec #117, while retaining ADR-0042's
Workspace ownership, source attribution, access posture, and consumer separation. Ambiguous
identity still requires resolution; automatic creation neither confirms the Workspace owner nor
authorises a person as an author. The twenty approved depth requirements describe dossier coverage,
not a questionnaire on the Profile.

The binding specification is [#204](https://github.com/nicolas-found42/chief-of-staff-demo/issues/204).
Issues #205–#212 hold the dependency-linked implementation and verification work; this decision
records the approved target, not a claim that those capabilities are already implemented.

Stronger, identity-matched research can supersede manual factual corrections when the evidence
establishes they are wrong or stale; original corrections, source versions, opposing claims, and
the reason for supersession remain inspectable. Retrieval recency is not factual authority, and
this priority never overrides wrong-person decisions, archive, deletion, or the privacy boundary
between public evidence and Relationship history.
